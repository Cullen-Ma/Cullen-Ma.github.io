👋 **Hello! I am Kun Ma (马琨).**

I am currently a Master's student at the **Institute of Medical Technology, Peking University**, majoring in Medical Imaging AI. I received my B.E. degree from **Xidian University** in 2023 (Rank 2/142).

My research interests lie at the intersection of **Medical Imaging** and **Artificial Intelligence**, with a particular focus on:
*   **Uncertainty Estimation** in Medical Imaging (e.g., PET)
*   **Multimodal Fusion** (PET/CT & Clinical Reports)
*   **Deep Learning** for Computer Aided Diagnosis

I am dedicated to developing robust and explainable AI algorithms to assist clinical decision-making.
